# DCLGAN stale launch-wrapper retirement

Three local PowerShell/SSH launch pairs for the 4090A DCLGAN evaluation,
addendum, and health processes remained blocked after their detached remote
commands had already succeeded.  The authoritative remote supervisors and
health watcher were verified live and healthy before any action.

The six local launch-wrapper processes were stopped after exact command-line
checks.  Their continued presence had no scientific role and created a future
duplicate-launch risk.  The local DCLGAN trainer, source exporter, push chain,
and their health watchers remained live; the existing remote evaluation,
addendum, and health processes also remained live and unchanged.

No checkpoint, sampler, RNG, training process, remote delivery process, result,
algorithm, or protocol was changed.  No paired performance was read and
confirmation20 remains sealed.  The retired wrappers must not be restarted;
the hash-pinned local and remote control supervisors are the authoritative
delivery chain.
