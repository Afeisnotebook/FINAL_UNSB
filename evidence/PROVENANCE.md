# Evidence provenance

Curated evidence was copied without scientific rewriting from the previous
local research tree. The previous canonical Git anchor was
`071246a5530cbb0b26081914e3692ed9d56142e4` in
`https://github.com/Afeisnotebook/UNSB_RESEARCH`.

Only compact adjudications needed to choose the final four lanes are retained.
Large checkpoints, raw pixels, historical orchestration trees and superseded
plans are intentionally excluded. `tools/hash_tree.py` produces the committed
evidence/source hash inventory after local preflight.

The upstream UNSB implementation is `https://github.com/cyclomon/UNSB`; its
MIT license is retained at `src/LICENSE`.
